# scribe.batch-admitted history

## 1.0.0

- Introduced the provider-neutral admitted-batch fact the Scribe coordinator emits when its governed admission policy closes a batch (`batch-complete` or `idle-timeout`, ADR-021). It carries the immutable `scribe_batch_identity`, the batch's new authoritative `new_evidence_segments`, the bounded `background_context` (`transcript_segments` + `prior_logged_items`) used for interpretation and duplicate suppression, and the `policy_profile`/`instruction_version` the batch was admitted under.
- The payload has no `model`, provider, endpoint, or `limits` field, and `additionalProperties: false` makes adding one a contract violation rather than a convention. The coordinator holds no provider or model knowledge (`Architecture/OperationalAgentRoles.md`); the extraction boundary supplies the model name from its own local configuration and the request ceilings from the governed protocol constants in `contracts/model-protocol.mjs`.
- Registered on the `domain` plane, following the existing `transcript.context-window` extraction-trigger precedent: an admitted batch is a session evidence fact, not AI-lane scheduling. `ai.work-request`/`ai.work-completed` remain the `control`-plane channel between the extraction boundary and the serial AI model lane, so one message type no longer serves two different transports and no consumer has to guard against its own emitted messages looping back.
